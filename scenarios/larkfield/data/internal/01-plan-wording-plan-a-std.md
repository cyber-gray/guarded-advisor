# Plan A: short-term disability (STD) plan wording

Larkfield Mutual (fictional). Synthetic document for the Guarded Advisor lab; not real plan wording.

## Eligibility

- Active employees of a participating employer who work at least 20 hours a week.
- Coverage starts on the first day of the month after 3 months of continuous employment.

## Benefit

- 70% of weekly insurable earnings, up to a maximum of CAD 1,500 a week.
- Waiting period: 7 calendar days for illness or injury; none for hospitalization or day surgery.
- Maximum benefit period: 17 weeks from the end of the waiting period.

## Definition of disability

The member is unable to perform the essential duties of their own occupation because of illness or injury, and is
receiving appropriate ongoing treatment from a licensed health professional.

## Graduated return to work

Benefits continue during a graduated return to work. Earnings from modified duties reduce the benefit so that
benefit plus earnings don't exceed 100% of pre-disability earnings.

## Claim decisions

Only a Larkfield Mutual case manager or adjudicator decides whether a claim is approved, continued, changed or
closed. Tools and assistants may summarize information for the case manager but never make or record a decision.
