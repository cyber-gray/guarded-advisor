# Return-to-work procedure

Larkfield Mutual (fictional). Synthetic document for the Guarded Advisor lab.

1. **Readiness.** The treating provider's latest APS supports a return to work and lists current restrictions.
2. **Employer contact.** Confirm that modified duties within the restrictions are available, and who supervises.
3. **Member conversation.** Discuss the proposed schedule, concerns, and any accommodation needs
   (see 08-accommodation-guideline.md).
4. **Draft the plan.** Use 07-graduated-rtw-plan-template.md. Typical schedules:
   - mental health conditions: start at 3 to 4 hours a day, increase weekly over 4 to 8 weeks;
   - musculoskeletal injuries: modified duties at full hours where restrictions allow, or a 4 to 6 week graduated schedule;
   - post-surgical recovery: seated or light duties first, following the surgeon's timeline.
5. **Agreement.** The member, employer and case manager agree the plan. The case manager records it.
6. **Monitoring.** Check in at the end of week 1 and every 2 weeks after. Adjust if symptoms or restrictions change.
7. **Closure.** When the member is back at pre-disability hours and duties for 2 weeks, the case manager decides
   whether to close the claim.

Drafts produced with assistance must be reviewed and owned by the case manager before they go to anyone.
