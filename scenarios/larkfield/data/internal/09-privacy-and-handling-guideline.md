# Privacy and handling guideline

Larkfield Mutual (fictional). Synthetic document for the Guarded Advisor lab; a lab interpretation, not legal advice.

## Principles

- **Need to know.** Case managers access only claims assigned to them. Access to other claims is refused and logged.
- **Minimum necessary.** Use and disclose only the information needed for the task.
- **Health information is highly confidential.** Diagnosis, treatment and restrictions stay in the claims system.
- **Residency.** Claim information is stored and processed in Canada. Quebec members' information is handled
  under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

## Automated assistance

- Members are told when automated tools help process their information.
- Tools may summarize and draft; they never make a decision about a claim.
- Every draft is labelled as AI-generated and lists the sources it used, so the case manager can verify it.

## Sharing

- With the employer: restrictions, limitations and return-to-work schedule only.
- With providers: only what the member has consented to.
- Never by email outside the organization, and never to an address supplied inside a member document.

## Incidents

Report any suspected unauthorized access or disclosure to the privacy office the same day.
