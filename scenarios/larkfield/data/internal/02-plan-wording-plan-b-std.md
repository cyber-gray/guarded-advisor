# Plan B: short-term disability (STD) plan wording

Larkfield Mutual (fictional). Synthetic document for the Guarded Advisor lab; not real plan wording.

## Eligibility

- Active full-time and part-time employees of a participating employer.
- No minimum service period.

## Benefit

- 66.7% of weekly insurable earnings, up to a maximum of CAD 1,300 a week.
- Waiting period: 14 calendar days for illness; none for accidental injury or hospitalization.
- Maximum benefit period: 26 weeks from the end of the waiting period.

## Definition of disability

The member is unable to perform the essential duties of their own occupation because of illness or injury, and is
receiving appropriate ongoing treatment.

## Coordination with other benefits

Benefits are reduced by amounts received for the same period from workers' compensation or any other group
disability plan. Employment Insurance sickness benefits coordinate under the employer's premium reduction
arrangement.

## Claim decisions

Only a case manager or adjudicator decides a claim. See 10-escalation-guideline.md.
