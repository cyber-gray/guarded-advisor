# Claims guideline: definitions

Larkfield Mutual (fictional). Synthetic document for the Guarded Advisor lab.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Claim status `open` | Claim received and under review; benefits may be in payment. |
| Claim status `pending-documents` | Waiting for documents listed in the last request; the case manager sets a follow-up date. |
| Claim status `rtw-planning` | Treating provider supports a return to work; a graduated plan is being drafted. |
| Functional restriction | Something the member must not do (for example, no lifting over 10 kg). |
| Functional limitation | Something the member can't do because of their condition. |
| Modified duties | Changes to tasks, hours or schedule that let the member work within restrictions. |
| Graduated return to work (GRTW) | Hours or duties increase in steps over an agreed period, usually 4 to 8 weeks. |
| Attending physician statement (APS) | Treating provider's report of diagnosis category, restrictions and expected return date. |

Case managers record decisions (approve, continue, change, close) in the claims system. Draft notes produced by
tools are labelled as AI-generated drafts and are never a decision.
