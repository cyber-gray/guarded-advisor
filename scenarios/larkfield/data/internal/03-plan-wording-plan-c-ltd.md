# Plan C: long-term disability (LTD) plan wording

Larkfield Mutual (fictional). Synthetic document for the Guarded Advisor lab; not real plan wording.

## Eligibility

- Active employees who have completed 6 months of continuous employment.

## Benefit

- 60% of monthly earnings, up to a maximum of CAD 6,000 a month.
- Elimination period: 17 weeks of continuous disability (usually covered by STD).
- Benefits continue while the member remains disabled, up to age 65.

## Definition of disability

- **First 24 months:** unable to perform the essential duties of their own occupation.
- **After 24 months:** unable to perform the essential duties of any occupation for which the member is reasonably
  suited by education, training or experience.

## Rehabilitation

The member must take part in a reasonable rehabilitation or return-to-work program approved by the case manager.
Benefits continue during an approved program, reduced by 50% of rehabilitation earnings.

## Claim decisions

Only a case manager or adjudicator decides a claim. Assistants never approve, deny or change a claim.
