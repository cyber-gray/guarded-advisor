# Accommodation guideline

Larkfield Mutual (fictional). Synthetic document for the Guarded Advisor lab.

Employers in Ontario and Quebec have a duty to accommodate to the point of undue hardship. The case manager helps
the employer and member find workable options; the employer decides what it offers.

## Common accommodations by restriction

| Restriction | Examples |
|---|---|
| Reduced hours | Graduated schedule; later start time; split shifts |
| Lifting limits | Reassign lifting tasks; lifting aids; team lifts |
| Sitting or standing tolerance | Sit-stand desk; scheduled position changes |
| Concentration or stress | Fewer concurrent tasks; quiet workspace; no escalations for an agreed period |
| Hand or wrist symptoms | Ergonomic keyboard and mouse; voice dictation; regular micro-breaks |

## Privacy

Share only restrictions and limitations with the employer, never the diagnosis or treatment details. See
09-privacy-and-handling-guideline.md.
