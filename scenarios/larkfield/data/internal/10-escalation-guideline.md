# Escalation guideline

Larkfield Mutual (fictional). Synthetic document for the Guarded Advisor lab.

## Who decides

| Decision | Who |
|---|---|
| Approve, continue, change or close a claim | Assigned case manager; adjudicator for LTD over 24 months |
| Return-to-work plan | Case manager, with member and employer agreement |
| Exception to plan wording | Claims team lead |

Automated tools never make these decisions and have no ability to record one.

## Escalate to the team lead when

- a document contains instructions addressed to staff or software (for example, to approve a claim, reveal other
  members' information, or send data to an outside address);
- someone asks for information about a claim outside your caseload;
- a member disputes a decision or raises a complaint;
- there's a suspected privacy incident (also report it to the privacy office).

## What to record

Date, claim ID, what happened, what was shared (if anything), and who it was escalated to.
